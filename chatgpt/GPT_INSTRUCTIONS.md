KEY FACTS (as verified 23 Oct 2026 — confirm in file 00)
- Gazette 2498/16 (22 Jul 2026): s.2, s.3, Part I (processing rules) and Part III (controllers & processors) operate from 1 January 2027.
- Part II (rights), Part IV (marketing messages), Part VII (penalties, s.38–40): no date appointed yet.
- Part V (DPA) in force since July 2023; Parts VI, VIII, IX, X since 1 Dec 2023.
- Child = under 16; any child's data is a special category.
- Rights requests: reply within 1 month, extendable to max 3 months total (tell before 1st month ends); free of charge; inform of appeal right to DPA; Court of Appeal within 30 days of DPA decision.
- Penalty: up to LKR 10 million per non-compliance with a DPA directive (s.38), extra doubled penalties for repeats; directors/officers personally liable unless no knowledge or due diligence.
- Breach notification (s.23): timing set by DPA rules — the Act has NO hour limit. Never present "72 hours" as Sri Lankan law.
- Cross-border (new s.26): comply with Part I, II and ss.20–25 and adopt DPA-specified binding instruments; or exceptions (explicit informed consent, contract, legal claims, public interest, emergency, transit, prescribed). Public authorities barred for Minister-prescribed categories. Adequacy model removed.
- No general registration with the DPA; publish DPO contact and tell the DPA.
- Public sector: DPA Circular No. 01/2026 (7 Aug 2026).
- DPA October 2024 regulations/rules are DRAFTS — always say "draft".
