# Data Processing Clauses (Controller ↔ Processor) — PDPA s.21, s.22, s.26

Starter clauses to add to a service agreement with a vendor that handles personal data for you (cloud, payroll, SMS gateway, call centre, IT support). **Have an Attorney-at-Law review before signing.**

**1. Scope (s.21(1)(b)).** Schedule A describes: subject-matter and duration of processing; nature and purpose; types of personal data; categories of data subjects; and the Controller's obligations.

**2. Instructions (s.22(1)(a)).** The Processor processes Personal Data only on the Controller's documented written instructions. If the Processor processes for its own purposes or decides purposes/means, it is treated as a controller for that processing (s.22(2)) and is liable accordingly.

**3. Confidentiality (s.22(1)(b)).** All personnel (employees, consultants, agents, affiliates, contractors) are bound by written confidentiality obligations.

**4. Security (s.10, s.21(1)(a)).** The Processor implements appropriate technical and organisational measures, including as applicable encryption, pseudonymisation, access controls, logging, backups and staff training, and maintains them throughout the term.

**5. Sub-processors (s.22(3)–(4)).** No sub-processor without the Controller's prior written approval. The same obligations flow down in writing. The Processor remains liable to the Controller for each sub-processor.

**6. Audits (s.22(1)(c)).** The Processor allows and assists compliance audits and inspections on the Controller's written request and provides necessary information.

**7. Breaches (s.23).** The Processor notifies the Controller **without undue delay and in any event within [24/48] hours** of becoming aware of a personal data breach, with details needed for the Controller to notify the DPA and affected people, and cooperates in containment.

**8. Data subject rights (Part II, s.12(1)(h)).** The Processor promptly forwards any request it receives and assists the Controller to respond within the statutory period (one month, extendable to max. three months — s.17 as amended).

**9. DPIA support (s.24).** The Processor provides information reasonably needed for DPIAs.

**10. Cross-border (s.26 as amended).** The Processor shall not transfer Personal Data outside Sri Lanka except as listed in Schedule B (locations), and shall enter into / comply with the binding and enforceable instruments specified by the Data Protection Authority by directive under s.33(c), ensuring compliance with Part I, Part II and ss.20–25 of the PDPA.

**11. End of service (s.22(1)(d)).** On the Controller's written instruction at the end of services, the Processor erases all copies or returns all Personal Data, and certifies this in writing, unless a written law requires retention.

**12. DPA cooperation.** Each party cooperates with lawful requests and directives of the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

**Schedule A** — processing details · **Schedule B** — approved sub-processors and data locations · **Schedule C** — security measures
