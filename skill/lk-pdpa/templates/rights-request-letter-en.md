# Personal Data Rights Request Letter (English)

> Under Part II of the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025).
> ⚠️ Note: Part II (data subject rights) has no operational date gazetted yet (as of 23 Oct 2026). You can still send this as good practice. Check dpa.gov.lk for the latest status.

---

Date: ……………………

To:
The Data Protection Officer / Manager
[Organisation name]
[Address / email]

**Subject: Written request regarding my personal data**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I, [full name], NIC [only if needed — last 4 digits are usually enough], phone [……], a [customer / employee / student / patient] of your organisation, make the following request(s) (tick as applicable):

⬜ **1. Access (s.13)** — Confirm whether you process personal data about me, and provide a copy and the information in Schedule V (purposes, legal basis, recipients, transfers abroad, retention period, etc.).

⬜ **2. Withdrawal of consent (s.14(1))** — I withdraw the consent I gave for [e.g. promotional SMS/email].

⬜ **3. Refrain from further processing (s.14(2))** — Please stop processing my data for [describe].

⬜ **4. Rectification / completion (s.15)** — The following data is inaccurate / incomplete:
   Incorrect: …………………… → Correct: ……………………

⬜ **5. Erasure (s.16)** — Ground: ⬜ processed unlawfully ⬜ consent withdrawn ⬜ required by law / court order

⬜ **6. Review of an automated decision (s.18)** — Please have a person review the automated decision of [date] regarding [e.g. rejection of my loan application].

Under amended s.17, please reply in writing **within one month** of receiving this request. If you need an extension (total not exceeding three months), please tell me with reasons before the first month ends. If you refuse, please give reasons and inform me of my right to appeal to the Data Protection Authority. This request should be handled free of charge.

If you need additional information to verify my identity, please let me know.

Preferred reply: ⬜ email [……] ⬜ post

Yours faithfully,

……………………
[Name and signature]

*(If made by a representative — parent, guardian, person authorised in writing, or heir of a deceased person within 10 years of death — attach proof of authority. s.17(5).)*
