**Law:** PDPA s.24 (as amended 2025) and s.25. Operational 1 Jan 2027. Form and manner "as may be prescribed" — the DPA published **draft** DPIA Regulations (1 Oct 2024). When final regulations are issued, align this template with them.

## When is a DPIA required? (s.24(1)) — tick
⬜ (a) Systematic and extensive evaluation of personal data or special categories, including **profiling** (credit scoring, AI risk scoring, employee performance analytics)
⬜ (b) Systematic monitoring of **publicly accessible areas** (CCTV, ANPR, face recognition) or **telecommunication networks**
⬜ (c) Other processing determined by DPA rules
⬜ Processing relates to **national security, public order or public health** → **must also consult the DPA** (s.25(7))
