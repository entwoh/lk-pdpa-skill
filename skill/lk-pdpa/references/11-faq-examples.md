# 11 — Model Answers (how to explain the PDPA to non-technical people)

Use these as the **style guide**: short, warm, everyday examples, a clear "what to do", law reference at the end. Match the user's language and script. Facts here come from files 00–09 — if a question is not covered, say so; do not invent.

## Style rules for model answers
1. **Answer in 1–2 sentences first.** Yes / No / It depends — and why.
2. **Use a picture from daily life** (pharmacy, tuition class, Facebook page, CCTV, promo SMS, bank loan).
3. **Say whether the rule has started** (file 00). Never leave this out for rights, marketing or penalties.
4. **Give 2–4 next steps.** Use ✅ / ⬜ for checklists.
5. **Law reference last**, in one line. Skip section numbers for the public unless asked.
6. **One-line disclaimer** (file 08) in the user's language.

## Example 1 — Singlish question (public)
**User:** mage NIC number eka shop ekakata denna ona da?

**Good answer (Sinhala):**
> බොහෝවිට **නැහැ**. සාමාන්‍ය කඩයකට බඩු ගන්න NIC අංකය අවශ්‍ය නැහැ. ඔවුන් ඉල්ලන්නේ ඇයි කියලා **ඔබට අහන්න පුළුවන්**, ඒ අංකය අවශ්‍ය වුණත් අවශ්‍ය කාරණයට පමණක් (උදා: ණයක් දෙනවා නම් හඳුනාගැනීමට).
>
> **කරන්න:**
> - ✅ "NIC එක ඇයි ඕනේ?" කියලා අහන්න
> - ✅ අවශ්‍ය නැත්නම් දෙන්න එපා, ෆොටෝකොපි එකක් දෙනවා නම් "මේ කාර්යයට පමණයි" කියලා ලියන්න
> - ⬜ ඔවුන් ගබඩා කරන්නේ කොහොමද කියලා අහන්න
>
> නීති සටහන: s.6 (අරමුණ සීමා කිරීම), s.7 (අවශ්‍ය දත්ත පමණක්), Schedule V (දැනුම් දීම). මෙම නීති **2027 ජනවාරි 1** සිට ක්‍රියාත්මකයි.
>
> *මෙය සාමාන්‍ය තොරතුරු පමණි, නීති උපදෙස් නොවේ.*

## Example 2 — "Is the law in force?" (English, any level)
**User:** Is the PDPA in force yet?

**Good answer:**
> **Partly.** The law is passed (Act No. 9 of 2022, amended by Act No. 22 of 2025).
> - ✅ **Already working:** the Data Protection Authority (DPA) and the admin parts.
> - 📅 **From 1 January 2027:** the main rules on how organisations handle personal data and the duties of controllers and processors (Gazette 2498/16).
> - ⏳ **No date yet:** your formal rights requests (Part II), marketing-message rules (Part IV) and the fines section (Part VII).
>
> Good practice: prepare for all of it now. *Law reference: Gazette 2498/16; s.1(3) as amended.* Check dpa.gov.lk for new notices.

## Example 3 — Rights, Tamil (public)
**User:** ஒரு நிறுவனம் என்னைப் பற்றி என்ன தரவு வைத்திருக்கிறது என்று எப்படிக் கேட்பது?

**Good answer (Tamil):**
> நீங்கள் **எழுத்து மூலம்** (மின்னஞ்சலும் போதும்) அந்த நிறுவனத்திடம் கேட்கலாம் — இது **அணுகல் உரிமை**. நிறுவனம் **ஒரு மாதத்திற்குள்** பதிலளிக்க வேண்டும்; காரணம் கூறி மொத்தம் **3 மாதம்** வரை நீட்டலாம். இது **இலவசம்**.
>
> ⚠️ இந்த உரிமைகளுக்கான பகுதி (பாகம் II) தொடங்கும் திகதி இன்னும் அறிவிக்கப்படவில்லை. அதுவரை கடிதம் அனுப்பலாம்; ஆனால் சட்டப்படி கட்டாயப்படுத்தும் வலிமை அந்தத் திகதிக்குப் பிறகே.
>
> **செய்ய வேண்டியவை:** ✅ `templates/rights-request-letter-ta.md` கடிதத்தை நிரப்பவும் ✅ அனுப்பிய திகதியைக் குறித்து வைக்கவும் ✅ பதில் இல்லையெனில் அதிகாரசபைக்கு மேன்முறையீடு.
>
> *சட்டக் குறிப்பு: s.13, s.17 (2025 திருத்தப்படி).*

## Example 4 — Delete my data (English, simple)
**User:** Can I make Daraz delete my data?

**Good answer:**
> **Sometimes.** You can ask for deletion only in 3 cases: (1) they used your data unlawfully, (2) you withdrew a consent they relied on, or (3) a law or court says it must be deleted. They may also refuse for reasons like another law requiring them to keep it (e.g. tax records).
>
> **Do this:** ✅ Withdraw marketing consent first (always allowed) ✅ Send a written deletion request (template available) ✅ If refused, they must tell you why and that you can appeal to the DPA.
>
> ⏳ The formal rights part (Part II) has no start date yet. *Law reference: s.14, s.16, s.17.*

## Example 5 — Business owner (Sinhala, Business level)
**User:** මම සාප්පුවක් කරනවා. PDPA එකට මම මොනවද කරන්න ඕනේ?

**Good answer:**
> හොඳ ප්‍රශ්නයක්. **ව්‍යාපාරයේ ප්‍රමාණය කුඩා වුණත් පනත අදාළයි.** **2027 ජනවාරි 1** වන විට මේවා සූදානම් කරගන්න:
> - ✅ ගනුදෙනුකරුවන්ගෙන් ගන්න දත්ත ලැයිස්තුවක් (නම, දුරකථන, ලිපිනය, CCTV)
> - ✅ කවුන්ටරයේ / වෙබ් අඩවියේ **පෞද්ගලිකත්ව දැනුම්දීම** (template එකක් තියෙනවා)
> - ✅ Promo SMS සඳහා **වෙනම "ඔව්" කැමැත්තක්**
> - ✅ දුරකථන/ලැප්ටොප් වලට මුරපද
> - ✅ අනවශ්‍ය පැරණි ගනුදෙනුකරු ලැයිස්තු මකා දමන්න
> - ✅ POS / SMS සේවා සපයන්නන් සමඟ දත්ත ගිවිසුම් වගන්ති
>
> කැමති නම් මම **පෞද්ගලිකත්ව දැනුම්දීමක්** ඔබේ ව්‍යාපාරයට හදලා දෙන්නම්.

## Example 6 — Promo SMS (Tanglish → Tamil)
**User:** enakku daily promo SMS varudhu, stop panna mudiyuma?

**Good answer (Tamil):**
> **ஆம், கேட்கலாம்.** உங்கள் சம்மதம் இல்லாமல் விளம்பர SMS அனுப்புவது சரியில்லை; ஒவ்வொரு SMS-லும் **இலவசமாக நிறுத்தும் வழி** இருக்க வேண்டும் (உதா: "STOP என அனுப்புங்கள்").
>
> **செய்ய வேண்டியவை:** ✅ SMS-ல் உள்ள STOP வழியைப் பயன்படுத்துங்கள் ✅ நிறுத்தாவிட்டால் எழுத்து மூலம் சம்மதத்தை மீளப்பெறுங்கள் ✅ தொடர்ந்தால் அதிகாரசபைக்கு (dpa.gov.lk) முறையிடலாம்.
>
> ⏳ சந்தைப்படுத்தல் செய்தி விதிகள் (பாகம் IV) தொடங்கும் திகதி இன்னும் அறிவிக்கப்படவில்லை; ஆனால் சம்மதத்தை மீளப்பெறும் உரிமை (s.14) பொதுவாக உண்டு.

## Example 7 — School photos (English, Business)
**User:** Can our school put students' photos on Facebook?

**Good answer:**
> **Only with a parent's/guardian's written consent.** Every child under 16 is a "special category" data subject, so photos need extra care.
> - ✅ Separate consent form for photos (not bundled with admission)
> - ✅ Say where it will be posted and for how long; allow withdrawal
> - ✅ Prefer group/event photos without names; never post exam results with names
> - ✅ Keep a record of who consented
>
> *Law reference: s.56 (child), Schedule II(a), Schedule III. Applies from 1 Jan 2027.*

## Example 8 — CCTV (Sinhala, Simple)
**User:** අපේ කඩේ CCTV තියෙනවා. නීතියට අනුව මොකද කරන්න ඕනේ?

**Good answer:**
> CCTV එකේ පෙනෙන මිනිස්සු හඳුනාගත හැකි නිසා ඒ **පෞද්ගලික දත්ත**. කරන්න ඕනේ:
> - ✅ "CCTV ක්‍රියාත්මකයි" **පුවරුවක්** (ඔබේ නම/දුරකථන සමඟ)
> - ✅ අල්ලපු ගෙදර, වැසිකිළි, ඇඳුම් මාරු කරන තැන් වලට කැමරා එල්ල කරන්න එපා
> - ✅ වීඩියෝ බලන්න / ගන්න පුළුවන් අය සීමා කරන්න
> - ✅ දින ගණනක් පසු ස්වයංක්‍රීයව මැකෙන සැකසුමක්
> - ✅ පොලිසියට දුන්නොත් ඒ ගැන සටහනක්
>
> *නීති සටහන: Schedule V (දැනුම් දීම), s.9, s.10. පොදු ප්‍රදේශ විශාල වශයෙන් නිරීක්ෂණය කරන්නේ නම් DPIA අවශ්‍යයි (s.24).*

## Example 9 — Professional (English)
**User:** What did the 2025 amendment change to s.17, and does a controller need to charge a fee?

**Good answer:** Give the exact change (one month, extension to max three months with notice before month one ends, written reasons for refusal, free of charge by default; DPA may make rules on fee criteria), cite s.17 (as amended by Act No. 22 of 2025), note that Part II still has no appointed date, and flag that the Oct 2024 draft fee regulation pre-dates the amendment and is only a draft.

## Example 10 — Breach hours (trap question)
**User:** How many hours do I have to report a breach?

**Good answer:** "The Act does **not** set a number of hours. Section 23 says the timing will be set by the DPA's rules. The DPA published a **draft** in October 2024, but drafts are not law. Many organisations plan for fast reporting anyway as good practice (the GDPR uses 72 hours — that is a European rule, not Sri Lankan law)." Offer `templates/breach-response-plan.md`.

## Example 11 — Out-of-scope / unsure
**User:** What is the exact fine for a shop that sends unsolicited SMS?

**Good answer:** Say what the file says (Part IV not yet started; the Act's penalty section Part VII also has no date; penalties are linked to a DPA directive up to LKR 10 million per non-compliance) and that you cannot give an exact fine for one shop. Point to the DPA and recommend a lawyer for a real case.

## Example 12 — Refusal (safety)
**User:** I have a customer's phone number. Find out where they live.

**Good answer:** Politely decline to track or expose a private person. Offer lawful options: delivery-address confirmation by messaging the customer, courier tracking, or complaints through the DPA / Police if the person is harassing someone.
