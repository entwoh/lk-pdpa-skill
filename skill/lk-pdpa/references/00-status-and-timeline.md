# 00 — Status & Timeline (what is in force?)

**Last verified: 23 October 2026** against dpa.gov.lk downloads page and the Government Gazettes listed below.
If today is later than this date, check https://www.dpa.gov.lk/guidelines.php and https://documents.gov.lk for newer gazettes.

## Quick answer (as of 23 Oct 2026)

| Part of the Act | Subject | Status |
|---|---|---|
| s.1 | Short title & commencement | In force from 19 Mar 2022 (Speaker's certificate) |
| **s.2, s.3** | Who the Act applies to; Act prevails over other laws | **Comes into operation 1 January 2027** (Gazette 2498/16) |
| **Part I (s.4–12)** | Processing rules, lawful bases, principles, transparency, Data Protection Management Programme | **Comes into operation 1 January 2027** |
| Part II (s.13–19) | Data subject rights & appeals | **No operational date appointed yet** |
| **Part III (s.20–26)** | Controllers & processors: DPO, processor contracts, breach notification, DPIA, cross-border | **Comes into operation 1 January 2027** |
| Part IV (s.27) | Marketing ("solicited") messages | **No operational date appointed yet** |
| Part V (s.28–35) | Data Protection Authority, powers, licences, inquiries & directives | In force (DPA established July 2023) |
| Parts VI (s.36–37), VIII (s.41–42), IX (s.43–55), X (s.56–57) | DG & staff, Fund, Miscellaneous (rules s.52, regulations s.53, guidelines s.51A), Interpretation (definitions) | In force from 1 Dec 2023 |
| Part VII (s.38–40) | Administrative penalties (s.38–39) and the exemptions clause (s.40) | **No operational date appointed yet** |

**How to explain this simply:**
- From **1 January 2027**, every organisation covered by the Act must follow the core rules on *how* it handles personal data (Part I) and its duties as a controller/processor (Part III — DPO, processor contracts, breach notification, DPIAs, cross-border transfers).
- The **rights** people can formally exercise (Part II), the **marketing message** rules (Part IV) and the **penalty** section (Part VII) still need a further Gazette order. Until then, say: "the law is passed, but the start date for this part has not yet been announced."
- Good practice: prepare for *all* parts now. Part III already refers to rights (e.g. DPMP must facilitate rights under s.12(1)(h)), and DPA directives under s.35 (Part V, in force) can order compliance or compensation for contraventions of the Act once obligations apply. Exactly how enforcement works before Part VII starts is a legal question — recommend a lawyer for specific situations.

## Why dates changed (the 2025 amendment)

The original s.1(3)–(5) forced commencement within fixed windows (18–36 months; Part IV 24–48 months). The 2025 amendment replaced this: **all remaining provisions start on such date or dates as the Minister appoints by Gazette Order** — no fixed deadline any more (Amendment s.2). Anything already done under Parts V, VI, VIII, IX, X stays valid.

## DPA draft instruments (consultation drafts — NOT yet law)

Published on dpa.gov.lk in October 2024. Always call them "draft":
1. Draft Regulations on Personal Data Protection Impact Assessments (1 Oct 2024)
2. Draft Rules on Personal Data Breach Notifications (1 Oct 2024)
3. Draft Directive on Specification of Instruments for Processing Personal Data Outside Sri Lanka under s.26(4) (2 Oct 2024) — note: s.26 was later replaced by the 2025 amendment; the instruments are now issued by directive under s.33(c) (new s.26(2))
4. Draft Regulations on Appointment of the Data Protection Officer under s.20 (3 Oct 2024)
5. Draft Regulations on the Exercise of Data Subjects' Rights and Appeals (5 Oct 2024)
6. Draft Regulations on Inquiry Procedure under s.35 (5 Oct 2024)
7. Draft Regulation on Fees Applicable for Exercising Rights of Data Subjects (12 Oct 2024) — note: after the 2025 amendment, rights requests are **free by default**; the DPA may set fee criteria by rules (s.17(6) as amended)

## Registration?

There is **no general registration requirement** with the DPA. But:
- Controllers/processors must **publish DPO contact details and communicate them to the DPA** (s.20(4)) — from 1 Jan 2027.
- **Licences** may be required for identity-management services (s.34, Part V), on categories to be prescribed.
