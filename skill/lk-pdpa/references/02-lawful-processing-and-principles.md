# 02 — Lawful Processing, Principles & Transparency (Part I, Schedules I–V)

**Status:** Part I (s.4–12) comes into operation **1 January 2027** (Gazette 2498/16).

## The 8 obligations of every controller

| # | Section | Obligation | Plain words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | s.4 | Comply with the obligations in the Act | |
| 2 | s.5 | **Lawful processing** — meet a condition in Schedule I (or II for special categories; III when relying on consent; IV for criminal investigations) | Have a valid legal reason |
| 3 | s.6 | **Purpose limitation** — specified, explicit, legitimate purposes; no incompatible further use. Archiving in the public interest, scientific/historical research, statistics are not incompatible (subject to s.10 security) | Say why you collect, and stick to it |
| 4 | s.7 | **Data minimisation** — adequate, relevant, proportionate to the purpose | Collect only what you need |
| 5 | s.8 | **Accuracy** — accurate and up to date; erase or rectify inaccurate data without undue delay | Keep it correct |
| 6 | s.9 | **Storage limitation** — keep in identifiable form only as long as needed (longer only for archiving/research/statistics, with s.10 safeguards) | Delete when done |
| 7 | s.10 | **Integrity & confidentiality** — appropriate technical and organisational measures incl. encryption, pseudonymisation, anonymisation, access controls, to prevent unauthorised/unlawful processing, loss, destruction or damage | Keep it secure |
| 8 | s.11 | **Transparency** — give Schedule V information and info on decisions on rights requests, in writing or electronically, concise, transparent, intelligible, easily accessible | Tell people clearly |
| + | s.12 | **Accountability — Data Protection Management Programme (DPMP)** | Prove you comply |

## s.12 — Data Protection Management Programme (DPMP)

Every controller must implement internal controls and procedures that:
(a) keep **catalogued records** showing how ss.5–11 are implemented (in practice: a Record of Processing Activities — see `templates/ropa-template.csv`);
(b) are designed for the **structure, scale, volume and sensitivity** of processing;
(c) provide safeguards based on **DPIAs** (s.24);
(d) are **integrated into governance**;
(e) establish **internal oversight**;
(f) have a mechanism to **receive complaints, conduct inquiries and identify breaches**;
(g) are **updated** through periodic monitoring/assessment;
(h) **facilitate data subject rights** under ss.13, 14, 15, 16 and 18.

The 2025 amendment removed s.12(2) and inserted **s.51A**: the DPA may issue guidelines, including on the DPMP and sectoral guidelines formulated by advisory committees.

The effectiveness of the DPMP is a factor the DPA considers when setting penalties (s.39(c)) — a good DPMP reduces risk.

## Schedule I — Conditions for lawful processing (any ONE)

(a) **Consent** of the data subject
(b) Necessary for a **contract** with the data subject, or pre-contract steps at their request
(c) Necessary for a **legal obligation** under any written law
(d) Necessary to respond to an **emergency** threatening life, health or safety of the data subject or another person
(e) Necessary for a task in the **public interest** or exercise of powers/duties under written law (incl. government circulars, directions, codes)
(f) Necessary for **legitimate interests** of the controller or a third party, **except where overridden** by the data subject's interests — **in particular where the data subject is a child**

(g) "Public interest" includes: health purposes (public health, social protection, health-care management); control of communicable diseases and serious health threats; official authorities achieving purposes laid down by law.
(h) "Legitimate interest" includes: data subject is a client or in the service of the controller; the person reasonably expects the processing at the time and in the context of collection; processing strictly necessary to **prevent fraud**; processing strictly necessary and proportionate for **network and information security**.

## Schedule II — Special categories (any ONE)

(a) **Consent** for specified purposes (unless a law prohibits even with consent). **For a child, consent = consent of parent or legal guardian.**
(b) Employment, social security/pension obligations and rights; public health, public safety, alert systems, communicable disease control, public health-care management — **as provided for in a written law** with safeguards
(c) Emergency where data subject is physically/legally incapable of consenting
(d) Data **manifestly made public** by the data subject
(e) Establishment, exercise or defence of **legal claims**, or courts acting judicially
(f) Purpose provided in written law or public interest (Sch I(g)), necessary and proportionate, with suitable specific safeguards
(g) Preventive/occupational medicine, diagnosis, care, treatment, health-care management — **by a licensed/authorised health professional**
(h) Archiving in the public interest, scientific/historical research, statistics — in accordance with law, proportionate, with safeguards

Remember: **any data relating to a child (under 16) is special-category data.** Schools, tuition classes, children's apps and paediatric clinics must use Schedule II.

## Schedule III — Valid consent (ALL four)

(a) Controller can **demonstrate** consent was given (keep records).
(b) If in a written declaration that covers other matters, the consent request must be **clearly distinguishable**, intelligible, easily accessible, in **clear and plain language**.
(c) Consent is **not freely given** if a contract/service is made conditional on consent to processing **not necessary** for that contract. (No "agree to marketing or you can't open the account".)
(d) Before consent, the person must be told they can **withdraw at any time**.

## Schedule IV — Criminal investigations
Processing for lawful investigations of offences/security measures only under applicable written law with safeguards; lawful if under the Code of Criminal Procedure Act No. 15 of 1979 or other law; safeguards may be prescribed.

## Schedule V — What to tell people (privacy notice content)

**At the time of collection** from the person (item 1):
(a) controller identity & contact details (and representative, if any)
(b) DPO contact details, where applicable
(c) purposes and the **legal basis**
(d) the legitimate interest, if relying on Sch I(f)
(e) categories of personal data collected
(f) if consent-based: right to withdraw and how, without affecting earlier lawful processing
(g) recipients / third parties
(h) any **cross-border transfer** intended
(i) **retention period** (or criteria)
(j) existence of rights under Part II and how to exercise them
(k) right to **complain to the DPA**
(l) whether providing the data is statutory/contractual/required, whether obliged, and consequences of not providing
(m) existence of **automated decision-making** incl. profiling, with meaningful information about the logic, significance and consequences

- New purpose later → tell the person first (item 2).
- Data **not** collected directly from the person → also tell the **source** and whether publicly accessible (item 4), within a reasonable period and **at least within one month**, or at first communication, or at first disclosure to another recipient (item 5).
- Exceptions (items 3, 6): person already has the information; impossible/disproportionate effort (esp. archiving, research, statistics — then publish the information); obtaining/disclosure required by law with safeguards; professional privilege or statutory secrecy.
