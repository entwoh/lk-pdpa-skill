# 07 — What the 2025 Amendment Changed

**Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025** — passed 21 Oct 2025, certified **30 Oct 2025**, published 31 Oct 2025. L.D.-O 6/2025. Sinhala text prevails over Tamil if inconsistent (Amendment s.16).

| Amend. s. | PDPA section | Before (2022) | After (2025) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | s.1(3)–(5) | Fixed commencement windows (18–36 months; Part IV 24–48 months) | **Minister appoints date(s) by Gazette Order**, no deadline. Acts done under Parts V, VI, VIII, IX, X remain valid | Explains why dates were moved; led to Gazette 2498/16 (1 Jan 2027) |
| 3 | s.12(2) | DPA "shall" issue DPMP guidelines | Repealed (replaced by general guidelines power in new s.51A) | |
| 4 | s.17(1) | Reply within **21 working days** | Reply **without undue delay and within 1 month**; refusals with reasons | Clearer deadline |
| 4 | s.17(1A)–(1C) new | — | Extension **+2 months, max 3 months total**; notify before 1st month ends; tell requester about **appeal right** | |
| 4 | s.17(2),(4) | Covered ss.13–16 | Now also covers **s.18** (automated decisions) | |
| 4 | s.17(6)–(7) | Fees may be prescribed by regulation; controller explains fee | Requests **free of charge**; DPA rules may set criteria for when a fee may be charged; (7) repealed | Free rights for citizens |
| 5 | s.18(1) | Impact on rights "under any written law" | "under **the Constitution** or any written law" | Fundamental rights count |
| 6 | s.19(1)–(3) | Appeal list didn't expressly include refusal of **access** or **withdrawal of consent** | Appeal covers refusal of access (s.13), withdrawal of consent (s.14(1)), stopping processing (s.14(2)), rectification, erasure, s.17(2) refusals, automated-decision review | Wider appeal rights |
| 7 | s.20(1)(a) | DPO mandatory for ministry, govt department **or public corporation** | Ministry or government department only | Public corporations judged under (b) |
| 7 | s.20(1)(b)(iii) | Risk-of-harm processing (general) | Categories **determined by DPA guidelines** | Clarity coming via guidelines |
| 7 | s.20(5) | DPO "ensure" compliance | DPO **advises** how to comply; "obligations or requirements"; "personal data protection" | DPO = adviser; accountability stays with the controller |
| 8 | s.24(3) | Seek DPO help "where designated" | Seek DPO help (always) | |
| 8 | s.24(5) | Submit every DPIA to DPA | Submit DPIA **on DPA's written request** | Less paperwork |
| 9 | s.25 | Prior consultation with DPA if risks can't be mitigated; DPA instructions; s.25(6) deemed safeguards | (2)–(6) **repealed**; mitigation "under this Act"; s.25(7) mandatory consultation for national security/public order/public health processing **kept** | |
| 10 | s.26 | Public authorities: Sri Lanka only unless categories permitted via **adequacy decision**; private: adequacy country, or compliance + DPA instruments, or exceptions | **Replaced.** Any controller/processor may transfer if it complies with Part I, II, ss.20–25 and adopts DPA-specified binding instruments (directive under s.33(c)); or exceptions (consent after risk info, contract, legal claims, public interest, emergency, **transit**, prescribed); public authorities barred only for **Minister-prescribed categories** | Adequacy model removed; cloud use clearer |
| 11 | new s.51A | — | DPA may issue **guidelines**, incl. sectoral guidelines and DPMP matters | |
| 12 | s.52(2) | First rules within 24 months of Part V | Within **36 months** | |
| 13 | s.53(2)(b) | Regulations identifying adequate third countries | Repealed | |
| 14 | s.56 | DPO = person designated under s.20 | Includes **outsourced third-party DPO** | Outsourcing allowed |
| 14 | s.56 | Public authority incl. statutory bodies and any institution established by law | Ministry, Department, Provincial Council, local authority, PC ministry/department — **excludes public corporations and Companies-Act companies** | Narrower |
| 14 | s.56 | Third country = country prescribed under s.26 | **Any country/territory other than Sri Lanka** | |
| 15 | Sch VI item 6(2) | Director-General summons Board meetings | **Chairperson** summons | |
